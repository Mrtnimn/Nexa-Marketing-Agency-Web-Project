Harbor & Field: a brand built for its next chapter

Harbor & Field came to Nexa with a strong product and a familiar challenge: their brand was not yet communicating the quality of the experience they delivered. We created a clearer identity and a digital presence designed to turn interest into action.

Our work connected audience research, positioning, visual identity, and conversion-focused web design. The result gave the team a confident story to tell across launch campaigns, sales conversations, and everyday customer touchpoints.

Nexa helped us find the confidence in our brand. They made the strategy practical, the creative distinctive, and the whole launch feel unmistakably ours.

Sara Wilsson

Designer

We began by mapping the customer journey and identifying the moments where clarity could make the biggest commercial difference. That insight shaped a flexible brand system and a site experience that guides visitors naturally from curiosity to enquiry.

Since launch, Harbor & Field has used the new platform to support new partnerships, recruit with confidence, and introduce its services to a much broader audience.
